# Payroost — Hackathon Demo Script

**Target length:** 2 minutes 30 seconds (maximum allowed by the hackathon: under 3 minutes).  
**Format:** screen recording with concise narration or clear on-screen captions. Use only assets/music you have permission to use.

## Story
A freelancer and client agree on an e-commerce website. Payroost structures the agreement, collects a milestone payment through PayPal Sandbox, then catches a request that falls outside the approved scope.

## Shot list

### 0:00–0:15 — Problem and product
Show Payroost dashboard.
Caption/narration:
"Freelancers and clients often agree work in scattered chats. Payroost turns that brief into a clear project agreement, tracks milestone payments, and flags potential scope changes with AI."

### 0:15–0:40 — Create agreement
Show new project form and enter e-commerce brief.
Click Generate agreement.
Show scope, deliverables, exclusions, acceptance criteria, and milestones.
Caption:
"AI structures the brief into an editable agreement. The freelancer reviews every term before sending it."

### 0:40–1:00 — Client approval
Open client review link in another browser.
Scroll key scope and milestones.
Approve exact version.
Caption:
"The client reviews and approves the same version. Approval is recorded against that version."

### 1:00–1:30 — PayPal
Open a milestone payment page.
Show amount/currency and Sandbox disclosure.
Use Sandbox buyer to approve payment.
Return to Payroost and show the server-confirmed capture state, without implying milestone acceptance or seller payout.
Caption:
"PayPal Multiparty Sandbox processes the milestone payment for the eligible onboarded seller. Payroost updates the payment record only after server-side confirmation and verified webhook processing. The Payment Operations table then displays the persisted transaction."

### 1:30–2:10 — Scope intelligence
Submit: "Add a referral rewards programme so customers earn points when they invite friends."
Run analysis.
Show classification and matching/excluded scope evidence.
Caption:
"When a new request arrives, AI compares it with the approved scope and explains what appears to be additional work. It does not make the decision or charge anyone."

### 2:10–2:30 — Change request and close
Show editable proposed fee/time and client change approval page.
Close on dashboard/project timeline.
Caption:
"The freelancer reviews the proposal and sends a change request. Payroost keeps the original agreement intact and records approved changes separately."

## Recording checklist
- Use a clean browser profile and fictional data.
- Ensure no API keys, email inboxes, or personal details are visible.
- Use a stable, deployed URL.
- Pre-create the project if the live generation/payment process is too slow, but still show the actual functional result.
- Do not fake a PayPal success screen.
- Keep the PayPal Sandbox disclosure visible.
- Show the app working, not just slides.
- Keep final video under 3 minutes.
